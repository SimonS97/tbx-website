# Official Publisher Homepage Digest

Accessed: 2026-09-28

## Findings

1. MCDM's official home page surfaces several distinct offers, not one universal flagship: Crows, Draw Steel, The Delian Tomb as a starter adventure, Crack the Sun as an epic campaign, and supplements. Each named item has a use-oriented framing and a direct external purchase or follow link. Source: MCDM Productions, https://mcdmproductions.com/ (accessed 2026-09-28).

2. Free League's official home page provides multiple discovery paths before product depth: genre paths (including Fantasy and Horror), play-context paths (At the Table, Online, Solo), and a get-started route. Source: Free League Publishing, https://freeleaguepublishing.com/ (accessed 2026-09-28).

3. Mothership's official product page promotes a core proposition first, then distinguishes Core Set and Deluxe Set by their intended buyer outcome. Later it offers a separate module-library route and labels that library as campaigns, rules supplements, and in-universe texts. Source: Tuesday Knight Games, https://www.mothershiprpg.com/ (accessed 2026-09-28).

## Limits

- These publishers are materially larger than Tales by Xero and operate their own shops or product ecosystems. Their page structures are evidence of discoverability patterns, not evidence of conversion rates for a solo publisher with DriveThruRPG handoffs.
- No public source reviewed here provides comparable A/B test results for one featured product versus three curated entry paths.
