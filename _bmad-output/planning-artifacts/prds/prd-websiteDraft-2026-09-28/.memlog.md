- (decision) Public launch on likely Vercel hosting; success is a high-quality portfolio and qualified discovery, not campaign-scale marketing.
- (decision) Primary visitor entries are DriveThruRPG profile/product links and organic Daggerheart searches; the experience must signal deliberate human craft immediately, then lead through an appealing image or catchphrase to product fit and a playable next session.
- (decision) Launch-inventory discussion distinguishes the full published catalog from the three maximum intent-led homepage routes; proposed candidates are Abythera, Weeping Rift, Daggerheart Item Bundle, and Amber Tide.
- (assumption) The Weeping Rift DriveThruRPG URL needs confirmation because the supplied URL duplicates Abythera's product URL.
- (decision) The planned 'Run something tonight' homepage route should support a second, vibe-led product choice across multiple published one-shots, while preserving a maximum of three primary discovery routes.
- (change) Existing UX assumption that touch activation of a primary route opens a product detail directly must be revised: activating the one-shot route may first reveal an accessible, deliberate selection state; product selection then opens the detail page.
- (decision) Thorns of Blossomtide is a published standalone One-Shot in the 'Run something tonight' vibe-led selection: a 3-5 hour early-Tier-2 investigation and estate infiltration with player-led resolutions.
- (change) Ephemera is a published, standalone-playable second Abythera mission and a strong current catalog work: 5-8 hours for Tier-1 characters, focused on moral ambiguity, faction politics, investigation, infiltration, and player-led outcomes.
- (decision) Karrhold will have an independent, indexable Works detail page with a prominent accurate notice that it is included with Abythera, avoiding a duplicate-purchase implication while supporting search discovery and a framework transition.
- (decision) Every public product detail page should include a concise product-specific Author's Note explaining the creator's intention, relationship to the adventure or resource, and the desired table experience; it supports human authorship and trust without replacing factual fit information.
- (decision) Product detail pages must place reliable playtime, recommended player count, and character level or tier when applicable directly with the title hook, before the scene image and deeper product content, so GMs can assess practical fit immediately.
- (decision) Product detail pages use a consistent but variable facts strip: display only verified, product-relevant facts such as playtime, player count, character tier, printable assets, or VTT maps; do not estimate absent values or render empty placeholders.
- (decision) Visitors arriving from DriveThruRPG directly on a product page must immediately perceive the creator as serious and quality-focused. The page must clarify the linked work, retain a clear return path to DriveThruRPG, and deliberately invite discovery of a related next work.
- (decision) Each public product detail page must provide one curated primary 'Next work' recommendation based on meaningful product fit, plus a secondary quiet route to the full Works overview; it must not show a generic set of competing product recommendations.
- (decision) About must be concise and credibility-led: Xero has played tabletop RPGs across systems for more than ten years, writes and runs original adventures and campaigns, and refines releases from in-person table play. It must not become a long biography.
- (decision) Ko-fi is a clearly labeled, low-pressure external information and optional support channel for occasional release updates; no popups, forced signup, recurring sales prompts, or aggressive donation promotion.
- (decision) v1 analytics must measure entry source, product detail page views, per-product DriveThruRPG CTA clicks, and engagement with the three homepage discovery routes including the One-Shot vibe selection. Ko-fi clicks are non-essential and only tracked if available without extra complexity.
- (decision) Analytics must be minimal and privacy-preserving: collect only aggregate discovery and outbound-click events needed for v1 metrics; prohibit advertising profiles, retargeting, behavioral marketing, and unnecessary personal tracking or identifiers.
- (decision) v1 public discovery surfaces must list and route only fully published works; no 'in preparation' placeholders or future-work paths are shown at launch.
- (event) PRD finalized
- (change) Confirmed Weeping Rift DriveThruRPG destination: https://www.drivethrurpg.com/de/product/551669/weeping-rift-a-one-shot-adventure-compatible-with-daggerheart; it is eligible for public launch discovery surfaces.
